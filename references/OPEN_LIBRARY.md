# Open engineering books and courses

[Engineering Statics: full web book and PDF](https://engineeringstatics.org/) · [Thermodynamics: full web book, PDF, and EPUB](https://pressbooks.bccampus.ca/thermo1/) · [Fluid Mechanics: full web text](https://farside.ph.utexas.edu/teaching/336L/Fluidhtml/Fluidhtml.html) · [Python Numerical Methods: full web book](https://pythonnumericalmethods.studentorg.berkeley.edu/notebooks/Index.html) · [Modern Robotics: author-hosted full preprint](https://modernrobotics.org/).

This library connects the handbook's 35 subjects to longer books, lecture collections, exercises, and reference data. **Access checked: 2026-11-06.** A successful check means the indicated landing page or web content was accessible and identified the resource. It does not mean every chapter, video, downloadable file, or exercise solution was inspected. Exceptions and download limitations are recorded below.

The repository contains original handbook chapters and links to external resources. It does **not** contain copies of these books or their PDFs. Free reading access does not automatically permit republication, adaptation, or commercial reuse; each source retains its own license and attribution requirements.

## Access limits and useful alternatives

- **Book landing pages versus downloads:** the statics, thermodynamics, heat-transfer, and convex-optimization resources offer full texts, but some large PDF retrievals exceeded the research browser's size/time limits. Use their web editions or publisher download buttons. No local PDF was downloaded or bundled for this library.
- **Heat-transfer author site:** R08 was identified from the authors' indexed listing and direct PDF route. The earlier 502 and large-file extraction limit are retained as exceptions; it is not labeled an entirely verified live reading session.
- **Controls alternatives:** [Åström and Murray's Feedback Systems wiki](https://fbswiki.org/wiki/index.php/Main_Page) advertises a complete second-edition PDF, but direct access returned a server error during this check. [University of Michigan CTMS](https://ctms.engin.umich.edu/CTMS/index.php?aux=Home) returned 403 to the research browser. These are useful official routes to retry, with R17 as the checked course alternative.
- **Energy book:** [David MacKay's Sustainable Energy — Without the Hot Air, Cambridge repository record](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/13b878b9-e259-4b57-9bb7-379a1193341a) was identifiable, but author-site access returned 403 and a later repository fetch timed out. Its full download was not verified here; use R23 for the checked energy course. Its historical quantitative assumptions would need updating for present-day decisions.
- **NPTEL:** R11 and R24 course identities were verified, but dynamically generated syllabus URLs resolved to a nonexistent `undefined.pdf`; guessed older archive paths also failed. Use the current official navigation. This library does not claim that every lesson download was checked.
- **Mechanics Map:** a prior timeout prevented live verification. R03 is the checked full statics-book route.
- **Commercial references and standards:** a contents preview is not a full book. For example, the [ASM Handbook Volume 18 contents PDF](https://www.asminternational.org/wp-content/uploads/files/27533578/27533578-toc.pdf) is only a commercial handbook's topic preview, as identified in chapter 34. No access to the full ASM, ASME, ISO, JIS, or other paid standards is implied.

For a first study sequence, use R01 → R03 → R04/R05, and in parallel R06 → R07/R08. Add R12 and R13 to build a small machine or component, then use R14–R19 when computation, electronics, and control become necessary. Keep the original handbook beside the full resources as a formula and assumption reference.
