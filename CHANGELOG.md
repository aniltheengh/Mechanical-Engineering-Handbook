# Release history

## 8 November 2026 — continuum, multiphase, and process-plant expansion

- Added chapters 42–44 and Book 11: continuum mechanics, multiphase flow, and process-plant engineering. The library now has 44 chapters, ten theory volumes, and one consolidated formula handbook.
- Added six worked examples and nine solved exercises, with numerical checks for deformation and stress, phase balances and settling, and steady/transient plant balances.
- Added gas-quality/void-fraction and stirred-tank response figures, with assumed conditions, units, and model limits.
- Extended the study route, open-resource library, formula compilation, and automated GitHub checks to the new subjects.

## 7 November 2026 — specialist foundations expansion

- Added chapters 36–41: fracture mechanics, composite materials, compressible flow, engineering acoustics, corrosion engineering, and welding engineering.
- Added Book 10, Specialist Foundations, and included the new formula sections in the existing formula handbook. All earlier chapter and book paths remain valid.
- Added 12 worked examples and 18 solved exercises with three numerical verifiers. Checks include independent integration, conserved fluxes, laminate load recovery, acoustic power consistency, electrochemical unit conversions, and weld heat/load balances.
- Added crack-size/stress-intensity and sound-distance figures in PNG and SVG, with model assumptions documented in the visual guide.
- Expanded the reading routes and study guide. Updated coverage limits to distinguish introductory treatment from advanced specialization and design qualification.
- Corrected chapter 08's ideal-gas differential notation from a comma to a multiplication spacing command in the expression relating enthalpy and temperature.
- Made the build/check scripts derive chapter and volume totals from the declared volume assignments, and added checks against stale README and catalog counts.

## 6 November 2026 — first public edition

- Published 35 original chapters, eight theory volumes, a formula handbook, 70 worked examples, and 105 solved exercises.
- Added the pumping-system capstone, four quantitative figures, an open textbook/course library, and GitHub Actions verification.
