# 02 — Hydraulic and pump review

- [ ] Actual internal diameter, roughness and temperature-dependent fluid properties used.
- [ ] Major/minor and vendor component losses included without double-counting.
- [ ] Clean/fouled filter and operating-state flow envelopes calculated.
- [ ] System curve intersects actual OEM Q-H curves in acceptable region.
- [ ] NPSHA evaluated at worst realistic case and required margin reviewed.
- [ ] Suction geometry and supports checked against specific pump OEM instructions.
- [ ] Fast closure/power failure, minimum pressure and surge risks evaluated.
- [ ] Motor absorbed power and drive conditions checked, not guessed from hydraulic power.
