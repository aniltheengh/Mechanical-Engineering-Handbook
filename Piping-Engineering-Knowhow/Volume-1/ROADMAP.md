# Expansion roadmap (future, NOT delivered)

The target of 3,000 entries mentioned in discussion is an **aspiration**, not a count of written material. Volume 1 has exactly 60 entries.

Future volumes can cover: pressure design and materials selection; detailed valve sizing and cavitation; three-dimensional piping stress and dynamic response; hydraulic networks and transient simulation; fabrication and NDE; industrial instrumentation; water-treatment P&IDs; sanitary/high-purity piping; chemical transfer and hazardous media; steam/condensate; polymer and FRP pipe; buried water mains; firewater systems; cryogenic piping; power plant; pipelines; compressors/turbomachinery; JIS/Japan compliance; Plant 3D automation and data governance; case studies.

Each new volume requires its own bounded scope, technical source audit, calculations/tests and explicit exceptions. New content does not retroactively turn older general guidance into project approvals.
