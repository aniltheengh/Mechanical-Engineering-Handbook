# Chapter 07 — Equipment loads and supports

> Scope: general educational engineering baseline, **not** a project-accepted design or substitute for the governing code and manufacturer documents.

<a id="pk-0025"></a>

## PK-0025 — Equipment nozzles are not pipe supports

**Engineering mechanism.** Pipe deadweight, thermal movement and misalignment impose forces and moments on vessels, pumps and exchangers.

**Decision procedure.** Provide independent load paths and flexible routing consistent with equipment allowable nozzle loads.

**Verification / closeout.** Record allowable nozzle forces/moments and analyze governing sustained, thermal and occasional conditions.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Zero clash does not imply acceptable equipment loads.

**Reference anchors.** [EXP](../reference/SOURCES.md#exp), [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0026 — Support types define restraints

**Engineering mechanism.** Resting, guiding, line-stop, hold-down and anchoring supports differ by which translation/rotation they restrain.

**Decision procedure.** Define support restraint directions in a support register before stress modeling or detailing.

**Verification / closeout.** Check support drawings against 3D positions and analysis restraint assumptions.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Labeling every support 'anchor' can over-constrain thermal movement.

**Reference anchors.** [EXP](../reference/SOURCES.md#exp).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0027 — Span depends on weight, stiffness and service

**Engineering mechanism.** Support spacing is governed by bending stress, sag, pipe geometry, material, fluid, insulation, concentrated valves and vibration.

**Decision procedure.** Use approved project span guidance as screening, then investigate unusual loads or deflection-sensitive lines.

**Verification / closeout.** Check empty, operating, hydrotest and maintenance states; verify structural capacity.

**Failure mode or limitation.** One universal support spacing table cannot cover every pipe and service.

**Reference anchors.** [EXP](../reference/SOURCES.md#exp).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0028 — Thermal expansion needs a movement path

**Engineering mechanism.** Unrestrained length change is approximately alpha times run length times temperature change; restraint creates reactions.

**Decision procedure.** Study natural offsets, guides, sliders and anchoring, then undertake flexibility analysis for significant movement.

**Verification / closeout.** Check expansion from installed to each operating extreme, including differential equipment movement.

**Failure mode or limitation.** A flexible hose or bellows does not make adjacent anchoring and guidance optional.

**Reference anchors.** [EXP](../reference/SOURCES.md#exp), [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.
