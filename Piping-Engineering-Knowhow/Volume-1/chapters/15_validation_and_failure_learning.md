# Chapter 15 — Validation and failure learning

> Scope: general educational engineering baseline, **not** a project-accepted design or substitute for the governing code and manufacturer documents.

<a id="pk-0057"></a>

## PK-0057 — Hydrotest may not reveal all failure modes

**Engineering mechanism.** A pressure test gives evidence for particular pressure-boundary integrity, not for fatigue life, thermal movement, corrosion or every weld defect.

**Decision procedure.** Combine testing with required examination, material control and design verification.

**Verification / closeout.** Confirm the testing and inspection plan directly from governing standard and project quality requirements.

**Failure mode or limitation.** 'Passed hydrotest' is not equivalent to 'fit for every operating scenario'.

**Reference anchors.** [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0058 — Failure analysis starts with evidence

**Engineering mechanism.** Leakage, cracked branches, repeated seal loss and support wear may share causes including load, transients, misalignment and chemistry.

**Decision procedure.** Collect trend data, operating history, photographs, geometry, material evidence and sequence of events before proposing a fix.

**Verification / closeout.** Use root-cause methods to distinguish initiating cause from damage symptoms.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Changing one component without understanding the load or chemistry can reproduce the failure.

**Reference anchors.** [HI-NPSH](../reference/SOURCES.md#hinpsh), [EXP](../reference/SOURCES.md#exp).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0059 — Temporary and abnormal conditions govern surprises

**Engineering mechanism.** Flushing, cleaning, draining, vacuum, startup/shutdown, emergency trip and winter layup can exceed assumptions used for normal duty.

**Decision procedure.** Create an operation-state matrix and evaluate pressure/temperature/flow extremes for each state.

**Verification / closeout.** Review with operations before design freeze and commissioning.

**Failure mode or limitation.** A single 'normal process condition' is insufficient for complete engineering review.

**Reference anchors.** [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313), [EPA-MAIN](../reference/SOURCES.md#epamain).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0060 — Closeout requires accepted evidence, not confidence

**Engineering mechanism.** A design decision is reusable when scope, assumptions, result, source, checks and unresolved items are recorded.

**Decision procedure.** Close an entry with a decision checklist and reference version; reopen only on changed scope, evidence, or error.

**Verification / closeout.** Audit calculation tests, applicability, references and owners of unresolved actions.

**Failure mode or limitation.** An AI-generated explanation, by itself, is not independently approved engineering work.

**Reference anchors.** [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.
