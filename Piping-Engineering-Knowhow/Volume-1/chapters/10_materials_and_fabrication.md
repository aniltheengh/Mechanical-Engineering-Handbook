## PK-0037 — Galvanic compatibility is a system property

**Engineering mechanism.** Disparate metals connected electrically in a conductive environment may corrode preferentially depending on electrolyte and area ratio.

**Decision procedure.** Evaluate material couples and decide whether isolation or a different material is suitable.

**Verification / closeout.** Review actual wetted material pairings, coating integrity and maintenance.

**Failure mode or limitation.** A dielectric fitting is not a generic cure for all corrosion modes.

**Reference anchors.** [AWWA](../reference/SOURCES.md#awwa).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0038 — Water treatment piping must consider chemistry

**Engineering mechanism.** Chlorine, ozone, caustic, acid and high-purity water have different compatibility concerns even when all lines serve a water plant.

**Decision procedure.** Define concentration, temperature, exposure cycles, cleaning and sanitization before choosing elastomers and plastics.

**Verification / closeout.** Check supplier compatibility charts for full service range and project testing rules.

**Failure mode or limitation.** A material suitable for ordinary freshwater can fail in concentrated cleaning chemicals.

**Reference anchors.** [AWWA](../reference/SOURCES.md#awwa), [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0039 — Plastic pipe design differs from steel

**Engineering mechanism.** Thermal expansion, creep, restraint, UV exposure and joining methods vary strongly with polymers and service life.

**Decision procedure.** Use the appropriate polymer-product code/manual, long-term pressure derating and manufacturer joint procedure.

**Verification / closeout.** Review sustained pressure/temperature, support, expansion and chemical compatibility.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Copying steel-pipe support spans and stress assumptions to PVC or PE is unsafe.

**Reference anchors.** [AWWA](../reference/SOURCES.md#awwa).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0040 — Welding quality begins at the specification

**Engineering mechanism.** Weld preparation, process, material combination, welder qualification and inspection requirements are controlled by governing codes/specifications.

**Decision procedure.** Provide weld map, joint details and approved procedures appropriate to the service.

**Verification / closeout.** Confirm applicable WPS/PQR, welder qualification, NDE scope and repair traceability as required.

**Failure mode or limitation.** A neat-looking weld or 3D representation is not evidence of acceptable integrity.

**Reference anchors.** [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.
