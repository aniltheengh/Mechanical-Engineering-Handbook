# Chapter 11 — Fabrication and quality

> Scope: general educational engineering baseline, **not** a project-accepted design or substitute for the governing code and manufacturer documents.

## PK-0041 — Fabrication tolerance affects fit-up

**Engineering mechanism.** Cut length, flange orientation, shrinkage, field measurements and fabrication sequence determine whether spools install without forced alignment.

**Decision procedure.** Allocate field-fit spools and realistic flange/nozzle tolerances before issue for fabrication.

**Verification / closeout.** Verify dimension chains, tie-in survey and installation sequence.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Using nominal coordinates as if they were measured as-built coordinates leads to field rework.

**Reference anchors.** [SWAGE](../reference/SOURCES.md#swage), [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0042 — Flanged joints require controlled assembly

**Engineering mechanism.** Joint integrity depends on gasket seating, bolt condition, sequence, lubrication and the specified tightening approach.

**Decision procedure.** Follow site-approved flange management and bolting procedures, rather than generic torque guesses.

**Verification / closeout.** Inspect faces, verify gasket type and orientation, document joint assembly where required.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Overtightening can damage gaskets/flanges; undertightening can leak.

**Reference anchors.** [B16.5](../reference/SOURCES.md#b165), [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0043 — Pressure testing needs a test boundary

**Engineering mechanism.** A pressure test encompasses a defined volume, test medium, isolation scheme, allowable equipment exposure, vents and pressure gauges.

**Decision procedure.** Prepare an approved test pack with pressure source, barricade/isolation, fill/drain plan and inspection hold points.

**Verification / closeout.** Check actual governing code's test requirements, calibrated instruments and controlled depressurization.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Do not infer test pressure, duration or acceptance solely from a generic blog or this encyclopedia.

**Reference anchors.** [B31.3](../reference/SOURCES.md#b313).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.

## PK-0044 — Commissioning is not simply filling a pipe

**Engineering mechanism.** New piping may contain debris, air, oxygen, cleaning chemicals and unverified control states.

**Decision procedure.** Define flushing, cleaning, venting, instrumentation checks, controlled start-up and discharge destination.

**Verification / closeout.** Use startup checklists linked to P&ID and equipment OEM instructions.

**Failure mode or limitation.** Starting a pump against an air-bound or obstructed system can damage it.

**Reference anchors.** [HI-NPSH](../reference/SOURCES.md#hinpsh), [EPA-MAIN](../reference/SOURCES.md#epamain).

**Entry state:** `BASELINE-RESEARCHED`; **project acceptance:** `NOT ASSESSED`.
