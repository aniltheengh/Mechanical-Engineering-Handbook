# Free thermal expansion — length-change screening

**Question.** How much would a 30 m carbon steel run grow if its temperature increases by 60 K?

**Assumed:** constant mean linear expansion coefficient `alpha = 12 × 10⁻⁶ K⁻¹`, uniform temperature increase, unconstrained straight run.

```text
DeltaL = alpha L DeltaT
       = 12e-6 × 30 × 60 m
       = 0.0216 m = 21.6 mm
```

**Interpretation:** 21.6 mm is an *unrestrained displacement*, not an estimate of pipe stress or an expansion-joint specification. End restraints, branch connection stiffness, guides, equipment motion and cyclic temperature changes require more analysis. See [Spirax Sarco expansion and support](https://www.spiraxsarco.com/learn-about-steam/steam-distribution/pipe-expansion-and-support) and PK-0028, PK-0029.
