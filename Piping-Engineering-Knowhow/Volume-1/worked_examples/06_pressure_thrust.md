# Capped-end thrust — basic axial force screening

**Question.** What net outward axial force does pressure apply to a flat capped end with internal diameter 100 mm and 0.6 MPa gauge pressure?

```text
F = Delta p × A = p × pi D² / 4
  = 600,000 × pi × (0.100)² / 4
  = 4,712 N ≈ 4.71 kN
```

**Interpretation:** This simple force may inform a load-path check but is not the thrust at an elbow or expansion joint, nor the complete support force. At elbows, tees and reducers, pressure forces and momentum-flux changes must be assembled as vectors; actual restraints, friction, pressure-balanced systems, joint types, fluid transients and support flexibility matter. See PK-0026, PK-0029, PK-0033.
