# Rapid closure — Joukowsky pressure screening

**Question.** What is an illustrative instantaneous pressure change for a 1.0 m/s velocity change?

**Assume:** water density `998 kg/m³`, pressure wave speed `1000 m/s` (illustrative; depends on pipe/fluid elasticity and support), sufficiently rapid event, no complex reflections or vapor-cavity formation.

```text
Delta p = rho a Delta v
        = 998 × 1000 × 1.0
        = 998,000 Pa = 0.998 MPa ≈ 9.98 bar
```

**Interpretation:** This is a **first screening estimate** of a wave increment, not an approved maximum pipeline pressure. It is invalid as a complete result when valve closure is gradual, wave reflections and boundary conditions matter, pump inertia controls deceleration, or cavitation / column separation occurs. Calculate both high and low absolute pressures with suitable transient analysis when the risk warrants. See [EPA pressure management](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2021-12/ds-toolbox-fact-sheets_pressure-management.pdf), PK-0034.
